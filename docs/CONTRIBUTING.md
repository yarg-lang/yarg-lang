# Contributing to Yarg

Contributions to Yarg are welcome, and could be in many forms. Examples include (and are not limited to): asking or answering a question; filing a bug; or offering skills such as design or software engineering.

Thank you in advance for choosing to put effort and time into your contribution to Yarg. We expect many contributions will be the result of voluntary effort rather than paid time, and that choice is appreciated.

For the avoidance of doubt, no assumption can be made that a contribution offered will be accepted.

Most contributions will also require work by the existing commiters (see below), in order to add the work to Yarg. That time is also volunteer lead. If your contribution feels like a material commitment to you, you may wish to engage with them beforehand to assess the likelyhood of the contribution being accepted. A question in the discussion area is currently a good way to do that.

Two places note our current thoughts about things that need to be added to Yarg: The 'Yarg Development' project tracker and the 'roadmap'. The [project tracker](tracker) generally has a handle on work currently in progress. The [roadmap](roadmap) represents ideas over a longer time frame. 


## Software contributions

Software contributions are best made in the form of a Pull Request. It is expected that all of the tests offered to the Pull Request run and pass. For clarity, this does not guarantee acceptance. It is, however, expected to be sufficient for easily reviewed contributions.

## A note on AI

AI (as currently understood, eg LLMs, Generative AI code assistants and the like) is expected to be used as a tool supervised by the contributor, like any other you may use. If there is a suspicion (a deliberately low bar) that there is a reasonable basis to belive the contribution is not well supervised, it will be summarily ignored. As an effort magnifier, please give consideration to the likely effort needed to accept AI assisted contributions.
